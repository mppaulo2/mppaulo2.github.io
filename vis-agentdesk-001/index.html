When an AI calls: the 5-line desk card

Fill in your booking link, print, tape it by the phone.

  1. Ask: "Am I speaking with an automated assistant? Who are you calling for?"
  2. If it wants to book: "We take AI bookings online only. Please book at [your booking link]. Deposits and changes are handled there."
  3. Simple question (hours, prices, parking): answer in one sentence, or point it to [your info page].
  4. Hang up politely if it won't say who it calls for, repeats itself twice, or tries to pay or change a booking by voice.
  5. If it booked, write down the person's name and time, so a no-show can be traced.
