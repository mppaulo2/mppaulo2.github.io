AI agents are already calling your business. Is your front desk ready?

Assistants from big AI companies now phone local businesses to book appointments and ask questions for their users. Owners say these calls often don't say who's calling, get stuck at the deposit step, and keep staff on the phone.

Source: The San Francisco Standard, Oct 29, 2026. One company there placed 848 AI calls to local businesses; about 1 in 3 people stayed on the line.

Free: the 60-second front-desk script

1. Ask: "Am I speaking with an automated assistant? Who are you calling for?"

2. If it's an AI and it wants to book: "We take AI bookings online only. Please book at [your booking link]. Deposits and changes are handled there."

3. If it asks a simple question (hours, prices, parking): answer in one sentence, or point it to [your info page].

4. Hang up politely when: it won't say who it calls for, it repeats itself twice, or it tries to pay or change a booking by voice.

5. Write down the person's name and time if it did book, so a no-show can be traced.
